    items:
      - question: Is Cypress-Fairbanks ISD a good school district?
        answer: |
          Yes by every major measure: Niche grades it A and ranks it #2 in Harris County (2026), it is Texas's third-largest district with nearly 118,000 students at 96 schools, and U.S. News ranks all 12 of its high schools.
      - question: What are the best high schools in Cy-Fair ISD?
        answer: |
          Bridgeland ranks #126 in Texas with a 97% graduation rate, Cypress Woods #163 at 98%, and Cypress Ranch #192 at 97% (U.S. News, 2026). All 12 district high schools carry a state ranking.
      - question: How much is a home in Cy-Fair ISD in 2026?
        answer: |
          The median listing price in Cypress, the district's core market, was $445,000 in June 2026 (Realtor.com). Community medians run from $265,000 in Canyon Village at Cypress Springs to $740,000 in Towne Lake.
      - question: Is Cy-Fair ISD or Katy ISD better?
        answer: |
          Both are A-grade districts. Katy ISD holds Niche's A+ and #1 Houston Area spot for a fifth straight year; Cy-Fair is the larger district at #2 in Harris County, and its top campus, Bridgeland, ranks #126 in Texas to Seven Lakes' #51 (U.S. News). Housing runs cheaper in Katy at a $389,999 median versus $445,000 in Cypress (Realtor.com, June 2026). The right answer is your commute and your campus list.
      - question: Which communities are zoned to Cypress-Fairbanks ISD?
        answer: |
          Most of Cypress, including Bridgeland, Towne Lake, Fairfield, Coles Crossing and Cypress Creek Lakes, plus Jersey Village and pockets of northwest Houston, across 186 square miles of northwest Harris County.
      - question: Is the Cy-Fair ISD area a buyer's market in 2026?
        answer: |
          Realtor.com classifies Cypress as a buyer's market in June 2026: homes sell at 98% of asking, inventory is up 7.24% year over year, and the median sits at 71 days on market.
      - question: Does the Cy-Fair ISD area flood?
        answer: |
          Risk is concentrated in bayou-adjacent pockets near Cypress Creek, and newer communities have modern detention and drainage. Check flood history per address before buying; we do this for every client.
      - question: How far is Cy-Fair ISD from downtown Houston?
        answer: |
          Most Cypress addresses sit about 25 miles northwest of downtown via US-290, with the Grand Parkway as the outer-belt alternative. Energy Corridor commutes are the most direct.
