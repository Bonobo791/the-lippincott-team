      - question: Is Tomball ISD a good school district?

          One of the best in Texas: Niche grades it A+ and ranks it #1 in Harris County for the seventh straight year (2026), and all three high schools sit in the top 10% nationally (U.S. News, 2025-26).
      - question: How much is a home in Tomball ISD in 2026?
        answer: |
          In Tomball, the district's anchor city, the median listing price was $415,000 in June 2026 (Realtor.com). Neighborhood medians run from about $272,000 in Willow Forest to $482,500 in Inverness Estates.
      - question: Is Tomball a buyer's market in 2026?
        answer: |
          Yes, per Realtor.com's June 2026 read. Inventory rose 11.5% year over year, homes sell at 98% of asking, and the median listing sits 42 days on market.
      - question: What areas does Tomball ISD cover?
        answer: |
          Eighty-three square miles across northwest Harris and southwest Montgomery counties, including Tomball, parts of the Magnolia corridor, and Creekside Park in The Woodlands, about 30 miles from downtown Houston.
      - question: How are Tomball ISD high schools ranked?
        answer: |
          Tomball Star Academy ranks #119 in Texas with a 98% graduation rate, Tomball Memorial #165 at 98%, and Tomball High #240 at 94% (U.S. News, 2025-26).
      - question: Is a Tomball address always zoned to Tomball ISD?
        answer: |
          No. Postal boundaries and attendance zones differ here: some Tomball addresses feed other districts, and some Magnolia addresses feed Tomball ISD. Verify zoning per address before you offer; we do this for every client.
      - question: How far is Tomball ISD from downtown Houston?
        answer: |
          About 30 miles northwest via TX-249, with the Grand Parkway (SH-99) connecting to IAH, roughly 29 miles, and to Katy. The average Tomball commute is 33.4 minutes (U.S. Census, 2024).
      - question: Does the Tomball area flood?
        answer: |
          Risk concentrates along Spring Creek and its tributaries; the creek's watershed serves about half the City of Tomball (Harris County Flood Control District). Newer communities carry modern detention. Check flood history per address before buying.
