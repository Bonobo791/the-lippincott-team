After nine successful years in education at high performing private schools and highly acclaimed CFISD, Katie decided to employ her skills and talents in the real estate world. Katie carried with her from education the mindset that clients should be prioritized over all else. Katie’s passion for education shines through as she educates her clients while helping them navigate the buying and selling process. Her steadfast will and determination prove to be tremendous assets for her clients in helping them get the best deal for their dream home. Strong interpersonal skills help Katie provide stellar service to buyers and sellers in order to earn their trust, referrals, and repeat business. A native Houstonian, Katie possesses extensive knowledge of the Greater Houston area. Katie loves spending time with her husband, David, and their four children. They enjoy spending time outdoors, competitive family games, family movie night, and camping.

As a Buyer Specialist, Katie's top specialties are first-time home buyers, relocation, and luxury homes. She serves clients most often in Tomball, Cypress, Waller, Magnolia, and Montgomery — and as a Sugar Land native, she knows that area and its surrounding communities inside and out.
