With over 14 years of experience in enterprise telecom and software sales, Jenny brings a results-driven, strategic mindset to real estate — backed by a career built on negotiation, relationship management, and guiding clients through complex, high-stakes decisions. Working with executives and enterprise clients has shaped her ability to communicate clearly, advocate fiercely, and manage transactions with precision and confidence. She understands that buying or selling a home is both a financial investment and a deeply personal decision, and she approaches every client relationship with professionalism, transparency, and a solutions-oriented approach.

A Katy native now serving Cypress, Katy, Fulshear, and Houston, Jenny combines deep local knowledge with corporate-level professionalism to guide clients through every step of the buying and selling process with clarity and confidence.

As a Buyer Specialist, Jenny's top specialties are first-time buyers, luxury homes, and new construction, and she serves clients most often in Bridgeland, Katy, Cypress, and Fulshear. Outside of real estate, Jenny loves spending time with her husband, Ross, and is a proud mom to Brooklyn, Presley, and Camden and is deeply involved in the competitive cheer community.
