Natalie serves The Lippincott Team as a Buyer Specialist, helping clients navigate the search, the offer, and the closing with confidence. Her top specialties are first-time buyers, luxury homes, and new construction — she enjoys demystifying the process for first-time buyers as much as she enjoys matching move-up clients with the right build or high-end home.

She serves buyers most often in Cypress, Hockley, Waller, and Tomball.
