Natalie Buenrostro is a proud Houston native who has called Cypress home for the past 16 years, giving her deep roots and a strong connection to the community she serves. Before entering real estate, she spent 18 years in education as a teacher, dyslexia specialist, and special programs facilitator. That experience shaped her ability to communicate clearly, stay organized, and guide people through important life transitions with patience and care.

Her strengths in communication, punctuality, and flexibility make her a natural fit for helping clients navigate the home buying and selling process.

When she's not working with clients, Natalie enjoys spending time with her husband Adrian and their three boys, Noah, Jacob, and Nathan. Their family life revolves around sports, especially baseball, and you can often find them cheering from the stands at local games.

Natalie is passionate about serving families in the same community she calls home!
