Stacy Hooper is a dedicated real estate professional specializing in luxury homes, waterfront properties, and expansive ranches. Known for her white-glove service and unwavering commitment to her clients, Stacy approaches every transaction with one priority: protecting her clients' interests and negotiating the strongest possible outcome on their behalf.

Whether buying or selling, Stacy is a strategic and relentless advocate at the negotiating table. She understands that the details matter, from price and terms to inspections, contingencies, repairs, and closing conditions, and she works diligently to anticipate challenges, protect her clients from unnecessary risk, and create opportunities that benefit them financially.

As a native Houstonian, growing up in The Woodlands, living in the Galleria-area, Spring Branch, on Lake Conroe and 13+ years of living in Bridgeland, Stacy brings exceptional knowledge of Houston's neighborhoods, schools, amenities, and lifestyle. Her local expertise, market knowledge, attention to detail, and commitment to strong negotiation make her a trusted advocate for clients throughout every stage of the real estate process.

For Stacy, exceptional service isn't simply about making a transaction seamless. It's about making sure her clients have a knowledgeable, strategic advocate in their corner from the first conversation through the closing table.
