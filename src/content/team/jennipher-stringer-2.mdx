Jennipher Stringer is a native Texan, born and raised in Lufkin, Texas. She moved to the Houston area in 1995 and has called Northwest Houston home ever since, living in Cypress for over 18 years before settling into the Waller area.

Before launching her real estate career, Jennipher spent more than 20 years as an Executive Assistant for Dow Chemical, supporting high-level executives and business leaders. That experience helped her develop exceptional organizational, communication, and customer service skills that now benefit her real estate clients every day.

For the past five years, Jennipher has proudly served buyers, sellers, and investors as a Realtor with The Lippincott Team and has worked diligently to help clients achieve their real estate goals with confidence. Real estate allows her to combine her love of helping people with her strong business background and she is committed to providing outstanding service, clear communication, and a seamless experience throughout every transaction she has.

Outside of real estate, family is at the center of her life and she is the proud mom to 3 adult children and their spouses and now a "Yay Yay" to 4 grandchildren. She treasures every opportunity to spend time with them, whether it's celebrating milestones, attending activities, or simply making memories together. She also enjoys networking, building relationships, and staying active in her community.

Jennipher treats every client as if they are family and is dedicated to making every real estate experience as smooth and successful as possible, and looks forward to helping her clients navigate one of life's most important investments.
