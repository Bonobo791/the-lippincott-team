Hope Renae is the Transaction Coordinator for The Lippincott Team at eXp Realty — the person who keeps every sale moving smoothly from contract to closing.

Once a home goes under contract, Hope takes over the details: tracking deadlines, managing paperwork, ordering and reviewing title work, and coordinating inspections and appraisals. She keeps clients, agents, lenders, and title partners on the same page with clear, timely communication, so nothing slips through the cracks and everyone always knows what comes next.

Her behind-the-scenes organization is a big part of why The Lippincott Team closes deals on time — and why clients consistently rate the experience five stars. Whether it is your first home or your fifth, Hope makes the final stretch to closing the easiest part of the process.
