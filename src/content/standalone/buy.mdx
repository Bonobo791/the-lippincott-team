faq:
  heading: Buying, **answered.**
  items:
    - question: Do I have to pay my buyer's agent out of pocket?
      answer: |
        Not necessarily. Since August 2024, buyers sign a written agreement before touring, and it states exactly what your agent earns. Compensation is [negotiable, and can be as low as $0](https://www.nar.realtor/the-facts/consumer-guide-to-written-buyer-agreements). Sellers can still pay it, and you can [ask for that inside your offer](https://www.nar.realtor/the-facts/nar-member-resource-dos-and-donts-when-working-with-buyers). In the June 2026 market, with closings at 95 to 98% of asking, concessions like this are on the table.
    - question: How much do I need for a down payment?
      answer: |
        Less than the 20% you have heard. First-time buyers put down a [median of 10%](https://www.nar.realtor/press-releases/first-time-home-buyer-share-falls-to-historic-low-of-21-median-age-rises-to-40) in 2025, and [92% financed their purchase](https://www.nar.realtor/press-releases/first-time-home-buyer-share-falls-to-historic-low-of-21-median-age-rises-to-40). FHA loans need [3.5% down with a 580 credit score](https://www.nar.realtor/fha/fha-loan-requirements). VA loans need [no down payment at all](https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/loan-types/purchase-loan/). Your written buying plan shows the exact number for your situation.
    - question: Is now a good time to buy in Northwest Houston?
      answer: |
        The data says buyers hold their strongest position in years: [5.3 months of supply in August 2026](https://www.har.com/content/department/mls?m=09&y=2026), [54 average days on market](https://www.har.com/content/department/mls?m=09&y=2026), June 2026 closings at [95 to 98% of asking](/northwest-houston-real-estate/#communities), and a [median price cut of $12,500](https://trerc.tamu.edu/reports/texas-housing-insight-june-2026/). The 30-year fixed averaged [7.03% on September 24, 2026](https://www.har.com/mortgage/rates-nearby). Nobody times a market perfectly, but leverage this clear is rare.
    - question: What are closing costs, and can I avoid them?
      answer: |
        Budget [2% to 5% of the purchase price](https://yourhome.fanniemae.com/calculators-tools/closing-costs-calculator) for lender, title and escrow fees. You cannot skip them, but you can negotiate them. Seller credits toward closing costs are a standard part of the offers we write in this market.
    - question: How long does it take to buy a house?
      answer: |
        The median search ran [10 weeks](https://virginiarealtors.org/2025/12/08/key-takeaways-from-nars-2025-profile-of-home-buyers-and-sellers/) in 2025. Across our six cities, active single-family listings had [median ages of 41 to 56 days in September 2026](/northwest-houston-real-estate/#communities), so you can inspect and negotiate without panic. Most of our buyers close 30 to 45 days after contract.
    - question: Can I look at homes before signing anything?
      answer: |
        Yes. Open houses need no paperwork, ever. A written agreement is required before private tours with an agent, nationally [since August 17, 2024](https://www.nar.realtor/the-facts/consumer-guide-to-written-buyer-agreements). Ours comes with a plain-English walkthrough first, and it is negotiable. If we are not a fit, you walk away.
