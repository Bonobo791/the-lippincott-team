faq:
  heading: Selling, **answered.**
  items:
    - question: How much is my home worth?
      answer: |
        Your home's value comes from recent sold comps in your community, adjusted for size, condition and location. We prepare that analysis in writing, free, and you keep it whether or not you list with us. Online estimators use corridor-wide data; a valuation from a specialist who has walked your home is the number buyers and appraisers will actually test.
    - question: Is now a good time to sell in Northwest Houston?
      answer: |
        It depends more on your next move than on the headlines. August 2026 is a balanced market: [5.3 months of supply and an average 54 days on market across the metro](https://www.har.com/content/department/mls?m=09&y=2026). Single-family sales in Cypress, Tomball and Katy recorded median days on market of 28 to 31 in August, and [pending sales declined 3.5% year over year](https://www.har.com/content/department/mls?m=09&y=2026). The right question is what waiting costs against your next purchase, and we model both sides with you.
    - question: How long will it take to sell?
      answer: |
        Across the six cities we track, median days on market for single-family sales ran 28 to 69 in August 2026: [69 in Waller](https://www.har.com/waller/realestate/pricetrends) at the slow end, [28 in Katy](https://www.har.com/katy/realestate/pricetrends) at the fast end, with [Tomball at 30](https://www.har.com/tomball/realestate/pricetrends) and [Cypress at 31](https://www.har.com/cypress/realestate/pricetrends) in between. Add two to four weeks of prep before launch and roughly 30 days from contract to close, and most sellers should plan on a three-to-five-month timeline end to end.
    - question: Should I sell my house myself instead?
      answer: |
        If you already know your buyer, possibly. [Thirty percent of by-owner sellers sold to a friend, relative or neighbor](https://www.nar.realtor/news/real-estate-news/nar-2025-profile-of-home-buyers-sellers-reveals-market-extremes). On the open market the comparison is stark: agent-assisted sales closed at a median $425,000 against $360,000 for solo sales, and only 5% of sellers chose the solo path, an all-time low. Pricing, preparation and marketing are the three tasks solo sellers themselves rate hardest.
    - question: What does it cost to sell a house?
      answer: |
        Every fee is explained in writing before you sign anything: compensation, title and closing costs, and any prep spending we recommend, each with the expected return attached. The consultation and the written valuation cost nothing, and the data is yours to keep either way.
